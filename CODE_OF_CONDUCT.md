# Code of conduct

We pledge to make participation in AG-UI.AI a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

Contributors should be respectful, assume good intent while evaluating evidence rigorously, give actionable feedback, acknowledge uncertainty, and focus disagreements on technical claims and sources. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, threats, sustained disruption, or sexualized conduct are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these expectations. Enforcement decisions should protect community safety, be proportionate, and respect privacy.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owners through the verified AutoGenUI organization contact. Do not use a public Issue for sensitive reports. Maintainers will review reports fairly and avoid conflicts of interest where possible.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
