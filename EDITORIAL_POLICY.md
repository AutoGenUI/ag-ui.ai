# Editorial policy

AG-UI.AI is an independent bilingual Generative UI news and research publication. Source evidence, dated verification, correction history and measured freshness support trust; no unsupported claims of authority or market leadership.

Evidence-complete news and analysis may publish automatically. Read the original, confirm event and publication dates, identify the source category, write original Chinese and English summaries, attribute vendor claims and document limitations. Missing key facts, unavailable original sources and unresolved factual conflicts enter a review queue. A verifiable portion of an otherwise inconsistent source may be reported only with explicit caveats and without the disputed claims.

Merge reports of one event; maintain separate event, source publication, collection and last-verification dates. Correct in place with a recorded reason. Withdrawals retain an explanation and disappear from feeds. Trends cite events, limits and counterevidence. News volume is not adoption. No full-text mirroring.

Sources include official blogs, specifications, release repositories, original research and evidenced practice, with ongoing discovery beyond the initial directory. Source check failures are recorded. More than 36 hours without successful collection is visibly stale.

The older GitHub ecosystem watcher remains a supplementary source-drift issue reporter. Dynamic editorial operations are documented in public/automation.md and use owner-authorized MCP tools backed by D1. Reading a public Site grants no write, mail or subscriber-data permissions.

Email stays pending activation until Brevo free account/domain and real delivery acceptance pass. No new paid services or model APIs. No automatic upgrades, empty digests, tracking pixels or silent retry of uncertain submissions.
