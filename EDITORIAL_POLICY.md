# Editorial policy

## Mission

Help a developer understand the Generative UI ecosystem in three minutes and make an explainable, source-backed technology decision without manufacturing a single-winner ranking.

## Source authority

- **Primary:** official specification, documentation, or repository maintained by the project.
- **Ecosystem:** an implementation, integration, showcase, talk, or secondary document describing a concept without a verified independent first-party specification.

Primary sources are preferred for factual claims. Ecosystem sources are useful for implementation evidence but must not be presented as official standardization.

## Review rules

- Separate the interaction, declarative UI, embedded host, and implementation layers.
- Describe maturity with evidence; avoid popularity scores and unsupported production-readiness claims.
- Explain both selection and avoidance conditions.
- Treat generated payloads as untrusted data and state the relevant validation, authority, and sandbox boundaries.
- Keep English and Chinese pages semantically aligned, not mechanically word-for-word.
- Record a real `lastVerified` date after opening every listed source.
- Never silently update editorial conclusions from an automated job. Automation may only open or update a review Issue.

Corrections that materially change a recommendation should include a short note in Updates.
